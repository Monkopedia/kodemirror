  `@replit/codemirror-vim` 6.4.0 does, on every platform. Hindi and other scripts whose vowel signs
  are Alphabetic but not letters are no longer split mid-word, and upper-case detection recognises
  astral capitals such as U+1D400 on wasmJs and native (#410).
