- Fixed accepting a snippet in `:autocomplete` (#383): the snippet's field positions were mapped a
  second time through the change that inserted them, so every snippet with fields either threw
  (`Position 9 is out of range for changeset of length 2` for `:lang-javascript`'s `for` snippet
  over `fo`) or put its fields in the wrong place. Template parsing now follows
  `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1: placeholder text (`${name}`, `${1:name}`) is inserted,
  numbered fields set the tab order, `\{`/`\}` are literal braces, and new lines are indented from
  the start line. An active snippet now ends when the selection leaves its field or a field's text
  is deleted.
