- Fixed accepting a lazy LSP completion (one that needs `completionItem/resolve`) misplacing or
  dropping the edit, or cancelling the editor's scope, when the document changed during the resolve
  (#434). The insertion and its `additionalTextEdits` are now mapped through the edits made while
  the resolve was in flight, and an auto-import whose range was edited is dropped, as upstream does
  for `additionalTextEdits` and formatting. A failed resolve or apply is reported through
  `logException` instead of being swallowed or failing the scope, and disposing the editor during
  the resolve applies nothing.
