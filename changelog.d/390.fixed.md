- Fixed the `:autocomplete` popup's typing and backspacing lifecycle to match
  `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1 on the same inputs (#390). A completion opened at the cursor
  (member completion right after `.`) no longer closes when you type and then backspace back to its
  start. Each result now keeps upstream's backspace limit, `min(cursor, from + (explicit ? 0 : 1))`
  taken when the query ran and mapped through later edits, and checks its own start, so deleting
  past `from` in one step, edits from other cursors, and results from several sources close where
  upstream does. A result whose `validFor` rejects the edited text, or that has no `validFor`, now
  re-queries its source, on backspace as well as typing, and an explicit session's re-query stays
  explicit. The selected option stays selected while typing re-filters the list.
