- `completeAnyWord` in `:autocomplete` now matches words as `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1 does
  on every platform. On wasmJs, Kotlin's regex engine classified astral code points by their low
  16 bits, so emoji runs such as 🤔🤔 were offered as words and CJK Extension B words were missed.
  Scanning a large document is also about three times faster on wasmJs (#407).
