- Fixed inline widget decorations (`Decoration.widget` without `block`) in `:view` being drawn at
  the start of their line instead of at their document position (#397). A widget at `pos` now sits
  between characters `pos - 1` and `pos` and pushes the following text right, as in
  `@codemirror/view`: widgets with `side <= 0` come before the caret at `pos`, `side > 0` after it,
  and several widgets at one position are ordered by `side`. The caret, selection, clicks and
  `coordsAtPos`/`posAtCoords` account for the widgets. This fixes the merge view's inline deletion
  markers and any custom inline widgets. `coordsAtPos`/`posAtCoords` on a line containing tabs
  also now account for the tab expansion.
