- Fixed default-keymap commands in `:commands` that crashed or corrupted the document (#380),
  checked against `@codemirror/commands` 6.10.2 on the same inputs. `deleteLine` no longer throws on
  the last line; `moveLineUp`/`moveLineDown` no longer scramble lines when cursors sit on adjacent
  lines; `transposeChars` swaps whole grapheme clusters instead of splitting surrogate pairs, and
  returns `false` when nothing can be swapped; `insertTab` indents a non-empty selection instead
  of replacing it. `copyLineUp`/`copyLineDown`, `indentMore`/`indentLess` and line-mode
  `toggleComment` now act on every selection range, not just the main one, and `toggleComment`
  places the marker at the block's minimum indentation and skips blank lines like upstream.
