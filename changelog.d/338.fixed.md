- `TreeFragment.applyChanges` and `TreeFragment.addTree` (`:lezer-common`) now match
  `@lezer/common`. `applyChanges` drops unchanged stretches shorter than `minGap` and marks
  fragment edges open as upstream does. `addTree` keeps only old fragments that reach past the new
  tree, and marks a partial parse's fragment `openEnd`. Incremental reparses previously reused
  nodes that upstream never offers, which produced different error-recovery trees on malformed
  input and different anonymous repeat grouping on large documents. (#338, #492)
