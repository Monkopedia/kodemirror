- Fixed `:autocomplete` filtering options by the text from a result's `from` to the cursor (#421).
  Like `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1, options are now filtered by the result's `from..to`, where
  an unset `to` is the cursor when the result arrives and then maps through edits. A result whose
  range ends past the cursor, such as an LSP `textEdit` range over the whole word, now narrows by
  that whole range, and accepting an option replaces the same range that was filtered.
  An async result that arrives after the user typed has its range moved through those edits, and
  typing past a result's `to` asks the source again.
