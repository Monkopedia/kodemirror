- `charCategorizer` and `wordAt` in `:state` now treat non-Latin letters (Devanagari, Thai,
  Tamil, astral letters and digits such as CJK Extension B) as word characters, and Unicode
  spaces (NBSP, U+2003, U+3000) as space, on every platform, matching `@codemirror/state` 6.5.4.
  Upstream's `\p{Alphabetic}` pattern never compiled on JVM or wasmJs, so double-click word selection,
  word motion and autocomplete word matching split non-Latin words there (#384).
