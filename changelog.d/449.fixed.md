- Fixed language data never reaching `EditorState.languageDataAt` (#449). Each `:lang-*` language
  now publishes upstream's `indentOnInput`, `wordChars` and `closeBrackets` data, and
  `StreamLanguage` publishes `StreamParser.languageData`, which was previously never read. So
  `indentOnInput()` reindents on `}`, `case `, `</` and similar triggers, and `wordAt`/word motion
  treat `$foo` (JavaScript), `background-color` (CSS) and `my-element` (HTML) as one word. Each
  case was checked against the npm packages. Comment tokens for LR languages still come from the
  `commentTokens` facet.
- Fixed `indentOnInput` deleting the typed character, and throwing `Selection points outside of
  document` at the end of the document, once a trigger matched (#449). The reindent is now applied
  as a follow-up change after the input, as upstream does. It also runs for `input.complete`,
  normalises the indentation string, and skips cursors beyond column 200.
