- Fixed `:lsp-client` applying server positions at stale offsets when the document changed after
  the request (#435). `formatDocument` maps its edits through text typed while the request was in
  flight and drops the whole format if that touched any edit. A completion's eager
  `additionalTextEdits` are mapped through the edits made before it was accepted, and one whose
  range was edited is dropped. They are now applied in the same transaction as the insertion, which
  used to land at its pre-import offsets. A snippet completion expands where its auto-import moved
  the text. Go-to-definition and find-references map a target in the same file.
