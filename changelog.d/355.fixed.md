- `:lezer-lr` parses over several ranges (mixed-language overlays such as `:lang-liquid` and
  `:lang-jinja` markup) no longer start tokens or error nodes in the gaps between ranges. The
  tokenizer now starts at the position clipped into the next range, and actions are applied at
  that token's start, as `@lezer/lr` does. This removes the spurious HTML `Text` nodes that
  spanned Liquid/Jinja tags. (#355, #472)
