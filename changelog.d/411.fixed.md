- `completeAnyWord` in `:autocomplete` collects words as `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1 does
  again: each document chunk of 1000 or more characters stops at 2000 words, and its list is cached,
  so a keystroke rescans only the chunk that changed. On a 1.16 MB document that takes about 1 ms on
  JVM and 3 ms on wasmJs, down from 19-116 ms. Matching `wordChars` no longer allocates a string per
  character on JVM and Apple native (#411).
