- Fixed the `:autocomplete` popup outliving the edit it was opened for (#383), as against
  `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1: moving the cursor, or accepting a completion with an `applyFn`
  (every snippet), left it open, so a later Enter inserted a stale completion; and a result's
  explicit `to` (e.g. an LSP `textEdit` range) was not mapped through typing, so accepting after
  typing left the typed characters behind (`foo barr)`). Edits away from the range now map it
  instead of being ignored, and edits touching it close the popup.
