- `closeBrackets` and snippets now match `@codemirror/autocomplete` 6.20.1 in four places (#395).
  `closeBrackets` reads the `closeBrackets` language data at the cursor (`brackets`, `before`,
  `stringPrefixes`) ahead of its `CloseBracketsConfig`. That adds triple quotes when a tripled
  quote is listed (typing the third `"` of `""` opens `""""""`, and a quote typed before a
  closing `"""` the extension inserted moves over all three). It also adds string prefixes such
  as Python's `f"`. Tabbing onto a snippet's last field now ends the snippet. Inserting a snippet
  and moving between its fields scroll the selection into view. A field number too large for an
  `Int`, such as `${99999999999}`, no longer throws.
